# Code of Conduct

This project is a place to learn and to help others learn. Everyone taking part, whether in issues, pull requests, or discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the field;
- criticise ideas and code, never people;
- assume good faith, and ask before concluding;
- keep feedback specific and useful.

The following are not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information without permission, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **irfan@datacortex.in**. Reports are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and may block anyone who does not follow this code, temporarily or permanently.
