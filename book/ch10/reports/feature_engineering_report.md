# Chapter 10 — Feature Engineering Report

_Generated by `ch10_feature_engineering_selection.py`. Numbers are
macro F1 on a stratified test split, with ± standard deviation
across 5-fold cross-validation on the bundled dataset. Deltas are
relative to the Chapter 9 baseline (TF-IDF + logistic regression
on description + skills_normalised)._

## Conventions

- **F1** is macro-averaged across the five role categories (AI
  Engineer, ML Engineer, Data Scientist, Data Engineer, Data
  Analyst).
- **± standard deviation** is reported across 5-fold stratified
  cross-validation. On the bundled ~580-row dataset, the standard
  deviation is large enough that single-number deltas can be
  misleading; the ± figure is what makes a result trustworthy.
- **"Decision"** is one of: **Keep** (added to v2), **Drop** (worse
  than baseline), **Keep for interpretability** (no F1 lift but
  useful in the FastAPI response payload or downstream chapters).
- Numbers are produced by running
  `python book/ch10/ch10_feature_engineering_selection.py`; do
  not edit them by hand.

## Hypothesis-result table

| Feature group | Specific feature | Hypothesis | Result | Decision |
|---------------|------------------|------------|--------|----------|
| Title | seniority_level (ordinal) | Senior roles cluster in higher salary bands and predict role coarsely. | -0.004 F1 (within noise) | Keep for interpretability |
| Title | is_remote_in_title | Title-level remote flag adds signal beyond the structured `is_remote` column. | 0.000 (constant on dataset) | Skipped — no variance on demo data |
| Skills | skill_count | Total skill count proxies posting comprehensiveness; longer skill lists may correlate with senior roles. | -0.017 F1 | Drop |
| Skills | has_python / has_sql / etc. (binary indicators) | The ~10 highest-frequency canonical skills carry most of the discriminative signal. | -0.003 F1 (within noise) | Keep for interpretability |
| Salary | log_salary_annual_inr | Log-transforming a heavily right-skewed numeric feature improves a linear model's use of it. | -0.016 F1 | Drop |
| Salary | salary_per_skill | Specialised roles (few skills, high salary) pay more per skill than generalists. | -0.011 F1 | Drop |
| Salary | salary_in_band_for_city | Within-city z-score normalises out cost-of-living differences (Bangalore Senior vs Mumbai Senior). | -0.011 F1 | Drop |
