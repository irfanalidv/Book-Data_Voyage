# About the Author

Irfan Ali is a senior AI engineer with seven years of production experience and the founder of DataCortex IQ. He holds a Master's in Data Science and AI from IISER Tirupati.

He has fine-tuned LLMs at a Schneider Electric subsidiary, built the AI intelligence layer for a Hong Kong startup, shipped a voice-first wellness app, and built inventory software for a Nepal-based FMCG business. He also built StackSift (https://stacksift.in), an LLM pipeline and API that identifies which software products a B2B company actually sells, with cited evidence; it is the fourth case study in Chapter 23. He has published eleven open-source libraries on PyPI, among them RAGNav and ragfallback, and two peer-reviewed papers (IJAINN, 2025).

He wrote this book from Siliguri, in West Bengal.
